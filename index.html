JellyC fleet · three levels

Level 1 SERVER. Level 2 SYSTEM (or GROUP for externals). Level 3 is a process inside a system, or a named provider inside a group. Dashed frames are the VPCs: no app host has a public IP. Checked against AWS, the tailnet and the code on 3 Nov 2026.

Solid vs dashed Solid accent arrows are SYSTEM → SYSTEM hops, each with its own API key or ticket, over private DNS or the internal ALB: 1 WenningNo.2 as user · 2 Monitor Hub → Nautilus book · 3 Tolas trigger bots → Trading Monitor, across the peering · 4 Daily Report and Pulse → Nautilus noon book · 5 fleet SSO, Tolas redeems its ticket at Nautilus (Hub, Unified, Trading Monitor and Fund Ops do the same) · 6 Fund Ops → Nautilus through the ALB · 7 Nautilus pulls the Tolas users.json over SSH. Dashed arrows are a process calling a concrete provider; their color matches the caller. Dashed arrows in the Network / entry color are the way in: a Tailscale client reaches router a or b (an HA pair, one carries the traffic at a time), the router hands it to the internal ALB, and the ALB sends each port to its system, across the peering for Tolas and TAP. The Funnel on router a also sends /wenning straight to the WenningNo.2 bot. Admins and Grok Bots come in through the SSM endpoints. Every call to an outside provider leaves through its VPC's NAT gateway, the band at the bottom of each frame. Route 53 only answers names, so it has no arrows. There is no Nautilus → Tolas API call: Nautilus attributes Tolas trades to a strategy from the Tolas tag that each venue fill carries.
